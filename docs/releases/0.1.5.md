# LBPPC 0.1.5

## New

- **Sign in with your lbppc.com account.** Click **Sign in with lbppc.com** under Online in the launcher: your
  browser opens lbppc.com, where you sign in with your name and password or with Discord, and two-factor if you've
  turned it on. Then the launcher is signed in and the game goes online with it. The launcher never sees your
  password, and **Sign out** ends the sign-in. Signing in with your name and password in the launcher still works.
- **lbppc.com has accounts now.** Sign in on the website with the same name and password you use in the launcher
  to set your profile picture and bio, link Discord, turn on two-factor, follow players and see what they're up to.
  If you made levels on the original servers, you can claim them back there: staff check each claim, and approved
  levels show up as yours.

## Fixed

- **Taking a photo on an ultrawide screen crashed the game** (21:9 and 32:9). Photos now save as they do at 16:9.
- **Silent characters in Spanish, German and other languages.** The US edition only has story voices in English and
  French, so in other languages the characters said nothing. They now speak English instead.
