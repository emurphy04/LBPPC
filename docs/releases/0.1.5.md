# LBPPC 0.1.5

This one is a smaller update to the game itself. The big news is **lbppc.com**, which is now a home for the whole
community, not just a download page. Plenty of improvements and fixes for the game are on their way in the next
updates.

## lbppc.com: your account, your levels, your friends

Sign in at [lbppc.com](https://lbppc.com) with the same name and password you use in the launcher. There's nothing
new to sign up for.

- **Browse every level from your browser.** New levels from LBPPC players, Team Picks and the other lists from the
  game, and more than ten million community levels saved from the original servers. Search by title, or by creator to
  find everything someone made.
- **Profiles.** Set your profile picture and bio, and they show up in the game too. See anyone's levels, photos and
  what they've been up to.
- **Get your old levels back.** Made levels on the original servers? Claim them on lbppc.com with the PSN name you
  published under. Our staff check each claim, and approved levels show up as yours again.
- **Follow players** and see what they're playing, publishing and hearting, all in one feed.
- **Photos** from the community, and what's happening on the server right now.
- **Keep your account safe.** Link your Discord account to sign in with it, verify your email, and turn on two-factor
  with an authenticator app.

## New in the launcher

- **Sign in with your lbppc.com account.** Click **Sign in with lbppc.com** under Online: your browser opens
  lbppc.com, where you sign in with your name and password or with Discord (and two-factor, if it's on). Then the
  launcher is signed in and the game goes online with it. The launcher never sees your password, and **Sign out** ends
  the sign-in. Signing in with your name and password in the launcher still works.

## Fixed

- **Taking a photo on an ultrawide screen crashed the game** (21:9 and 32:9). Photos now save as they do at 16:9.
- **Silent characters in Spanish, German and other languages.** The US edition only has story voices in English and
  French, so in other languages the characters said nothing. They now speak English instead.

## Coming up

This release is mostly about the website. Bigger game updates are being worked on, with more fixes and
improvements for how the game looks and runs. The Discord hears about them first.
